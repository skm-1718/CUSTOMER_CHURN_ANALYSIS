# CUSTOMER_CHURN_ANALYSIS

End-to-End Customer Churn Analysis using Excel, SQLite, and Python featuring data cleaning, SQL analysis, dashboards, machine learning, and business recommendations.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn using **Microsoft Excel, SQLite, and Python** to identify customer behavior, churn patterns, and business opportunities. The project combines data cleaning, business intelligence, SQL-based analysis, and predictive analytics to generate actionable insights for improving customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Clean and prepare the customer churn dataset.
- Analyze customer behavior and churn trends.
- Build KPIs and interactive dashboards.
- Perform SQL-based business analysis.
- Develop a machine learning model to predict customer churn.
- Generate data-driven business recommendations.

## 📈 Key Insights

- The dataset contains **64,374 customers**.
  
- Overall customer churn rate is **47%**.
  
- Standard subscription has the largest customer base.

- Basic subscription shows slightly higher average spending than the Standard plan.
  
- Monthly contract customers experience higher churn than annual contract customers.
  
- Long-tenure customers also churn, indicating the need for continuous engagement.
  
- Customers aged **52–65 years** represent the highest churn segment.
  
- Customers with longer payment delays are potential churn risks.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Strengthen customer retention strategies through personalized engagement.
  
- Promote long-term contracts using discounts and loyalty rewards.
  
- Improve customer support and reduce issue resolution time.
  
- Monitor customers with frequent payment delays.
  
- Develop targeted campaigns for high-risk age groups.
  
- Reward high-value customers through VIP and loyalty programs.
  
- Use predictive machine learning models to identify customers likely to churn and take proactive retention actions.
